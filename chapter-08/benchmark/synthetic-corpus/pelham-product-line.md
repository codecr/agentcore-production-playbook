## Pelham-8 flow controller

The Pelham-8 flow controller is a precision instrument designed for regulating fluid flow in industrial and laboratory settings. This section provides essential technical details, maintenance recommendations, and warranty information to ensure optimal performance and longevity of the device.

### Operating pressure

The Pelham-8 flow controller is engineered to withstand a maximum continuous operating pressure of **30 bar**. This specification ensures that the device can handle high-pressure applications commonly found in various industrial processes. It is crucial to adhere to this pressure limit to prevent damage to the internal components and to maintain accurate flow measurements. Exceeding the maximum operating pressure can lead to malfunction or safety hazards, so pressure should always be monitored and regulated within the specified range.

### Calibration and maintenance

To ensure ongoing accuracy, the Pelham-8 flow controller requires a standard calibration interval of **36 months**. Regular calibration is vital for maintaining the precision of flow measurements and preventing drift in performance. Neglecting this schedule can result in inaccurate readings and potential process inefficiencies. 

If a Pelham-8 flow controller skips its calibration, it will display error code **K-226**. This error code serves as an alert to the user that the device may no longer be providing reliable data and that calibration is necessary before resuming operations. Addressing this error promptly helps avoid costly mistakes and ensures the continued integrity of the measurement data.

In addition to calibration, periodic maintenance is recommended to keep the Pelham-8 flow controller in top condition. This includes checking for any signs of wear, ensuring all connections are tight, and verifying that the sensor surfaces are clean and free of obstructions. Following the maintenance schedule and addressing any issues promptly will extend the service life of the device and maintain its performance standards.
