## Kestrel-4 flow controller

The Kestrel-4 flow controller is a precision instrument designed for regulating fluid flow in industrial and laboratory settings. It is known for its reliability, accuracy, and robust construction, making it suitable for a wide range of applications where precise control of fluid dynamics is crucial. This section provides detailed information on the operational parameters, maintenance requirements, and warranty terms of the Kestrel-4 flow controller.

### Maximum continuous operating pressure

The Kestrel-4 flow controller is engineered to withstand high-pressure environments, with a maximum continuous operating pressure of **63 bar**. This specification ensures that the device can handle demanding applications where maintaining consistent flow rates under significant pressure is essential. The controller's internal components are constructed from high-strength materials to endure prolonged exposure to high pressures without degradation or failure. Operating beyond this pressure limit can compromise the integrity of the controller and lead to inaccurate readings or permanent damage. Therefore, it is imperative to adhere to the specified pressure limits to ensure optimal performance and longevity of the Kestrel-4 flow controller.

### Calibration interval and error codes

To maintain accuracy, the Kestrel-4 flow controller requires regular calibration. The standard calibration interval is set at **11 months**. Regular calibration ensures that the controller continues to provide precise flow measurements and adjustments. Over time, factors such as temperature fluctuations, mechanical wear, and minor internal shifts can affect the accuracy of the flow readings. By adhering to the 11-month calibration schedule, users can mitigate these effects and maintain the reliability of their measurements.

Failing to calibrate the Kestrel-4 flow controller within the specified interval can result in performance issues. Specifically, a unit that skips its calibration will display error code **K-882**. This error code serves as an alert to the user that the device may no longer be providing accurate readings and requires immediate attention. Ignoring this error code can lead to inaccurate data collection and potentially costly errors in processes that rely on precise flow control. Therefore, it is crucial for users to be vigilant about maintaining their calibration schedule to avoid operational disruptions and ensure the continued accuracy of the Kestrel-4 flow controller.
